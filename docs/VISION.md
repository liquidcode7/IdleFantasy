# Vision

> Working title: **Realm Forge** (rename freely — this name is a placeholder)
> A fork of [Idle Fantasy](https://github.com/tristinbaker/IdleFantasy) — GPL-3.0

## What we're building

A chill, offline idle RPG that keeps the set-and-forget soul of Idle Fantasy but
gives every level, every session, and every crafted item a reason to exist.
Levels open doors. Crafting has identity. The world responds to who you are.

Inspired by:
- **Guild Wars 2** — discovery-driven crafting, ambient and rewarding
- **FFXIV** — specialist identity, dedication matters
- **LotRO** — recipe mastery through repetition

Idle-first, always. You never have to engage deeply. But if you do, the game
rewards it.

---

## The 8-Type Wheel

Every material, crafted item, and enemy has an elemental type:

**Fire · Ice · Water · Lightning · Air · Ground · Light · Dark**

Wheel (clockwise — each type beats the next two):

```
Fire → Air → Ground → Lightning → Water → Ice → Dark → Light → Fire
```

- Strong against the next 2 types clockwise (1.5× damage)
- Weak against the previous 2 types clockwise (0.67× damage)
- Neutral against the remaining 4 types (1.0×)

Multipliers are starting points — tune to taste.

---

## The 8 Regions

One region per type. Each has a distinct biome, exclusive materials, and a
signature dungeon. Travel between regions takes real time (a session, queued
like any other).

| Type | Region | Biome |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Fire | The Caldera | Volcanic wasteland |
| Ice | The Frostveil | Frozen tundra |
| Water | The Abyssal Shelf | Deep ocean trench |
| Lightning | The Stormreach Coast | Perpetual storm coast |
| Air | The Sky Archipelago | Floating islands |
| Ground | The Rust Canyons | Canyon badlands |
| Light | The Celestial Plateau | High altitude plains |
| Dark | The Umbral Hollow | Shadow realm |

Region names are placeholders. Pick what feels right.

## The Core Loop

```
Explore region → gather typed materials → discover recipes → craft typed gear
→ prepare for typed dungeon → clear dungeon → unlock deeper region → repeat
```

No step is meaningless. Every action feeds the next.

---

## Crafting — The Heart of It

**Principles:**
- No failure states. You never waste materials on a bad roll
- Auto-craft always available — set and forget produces standard results
- Deeper engagement produces better results, never required
- Discovery over recipe lists — you experiment, the game rewards curiosity

**Materials carry type and origin.** A bar smelted from Caldera ore is a Fire
bar. Gear inherits type from its primary material.

**Recipe discovery (GW2-inspired):** Recipes aren't handed to you. You discover
them by combining materials. Some legendary recipes require ingredients from
multiple regions.

**Recipe mastery (LotRO-inspired):** Every recipe has tiers — Apprentice,
Journeyman, Expert, Master. Earned by repetition. Dedication has visible value.

**Quality tiers:** Standard → Good → Fine → Masterwork → Legendary. Higher
mastery raises the floor and ceiling.

## What We Will Not Do

- Punish idle play
- Require constant attention
- Add ads, accounts, or internet
- Lose the chill ambient feel

The game is always doing something for you. You just decide what.
